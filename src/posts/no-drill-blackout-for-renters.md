## Before You Buy: Check Your Lease and Your Window

Three quick checks will save you from buying the wrong thing.

### What Your Lease May Say About Windows

"No drill" doesn't automatically mean "allowed." Some leases have rules about window coverings, such as what color must face the street, or whether anything can be attached to the frame or glass. Others say nothing at all. Whether you can stick tape to a window frame or press a tension mount into it depends on your lease, so read it, and if it's unclear, ask your landlord or property manager before you install anything. A quick email now is easier than an argument at move-out.

### Measure the Frame Depth, Width, and the Surface You'll Stick To

Grab a tape measure and look closely at the window:

- **Frame depth.** Tension-mount shades fit inside the window frame, so they need enough depth for the bracket. LazBlinds lists a minimum frame depth of about 1½ to 2 inches depending on the guide, and some Amazon listings state a different minimum; check the listing for the exact model you choose.
- **Width and height.** Measure the inside of the frame and the outside edges. Note whether you'd rather mount inside the frame or cover the whole thing.
- **The surface itself.** Adhesive products care a lot about what they stick to. Redi Shade, for example, says its adhesive isn't recommended on drywall, unfinished wood, varnished or lacquered wood, wallpaper, or paint applied within roughly the last 30 to 90 days. If your window trim is glossy stained wood, that narrows your choices before you start.

### Why "Blackout" on the Box Isn't a Promise

Each of the three picks in this guide uses words like "100% blackout" or "blocks 99% of light." Those are manufacturer claims about the material. How dark your room actually gets depends on how well the product fits: light can still come in around the edges, the top, or the bottom of the window. For a deeper look at why small gaps matter so much, see our [blackout curtains guide]({{ '/posts/blackout-curtains-sleep-masks-for-day-sleepers/' | url }}).

**What it's for:** finding out cheaply whether a darker window makes a difference to you.

This is a pleated paper shade that Redi Shade says is made in the USA from durable, recyclable paper. We looked at dark static cling film, such as Gila Privacy Control Black, and didn't choose it for darkening a bedroom. ## No-Drill Blackout Checklist for Renters

**Before you buy:**

- [ ] Check your lease, or ask your landlord, whether you can attach anything to the window frame or glass.
- [ ] Measure the inside depth, width, and height of the window frame.
- [ ] Note what the frame surface is: varnished wood, fresh paint, drywall, and wallpaper are poor matches for adhesive.
- [ ] Check the seller and the return terms on the listing (some custom orders from manufacturers' own sites can't be returned).
- [ ] Decide whether you'll raise and lower it daily or leave it up.

**When you install:**

- [ ] Wipe the surface with rubbing alcohol and let it dry (Redi Shade's instructions).
- [ ] Test adhesive or hook-and-loop tape on a small hidden spot first.
- [ ] Keep shades away from heaters and candles.
- [ ] Make sure you can still open the window, reach the lock, and get out in an emergency.

**When you move out:**

- [ ] Follow the manufacturer's directions for removing adhesive, and go gently.
- [ ] Compare the frame against your move-in photos.

## FAQ

**Are there blackout curtains for renters with no drill?**

Yes. You can hang curtains you already own on a tension rod inside the window frame, or use a shade or cover that mounts to the frame without screws. Which one fits depends mostly on your frame depth and what the frame surface is made of.

**What's the most renter friendly blackout window option?**

A tension-mount shade generally leaves the least behind, because nothing is glued to the frame, but it needs enough frame depth. Stick-on shades are the budget choice but can leave adhesive residue.

**Will adhesive shades damage paint?**

It depends on the product and the surface. Redi Shade doesn't recommend its adhesive on freshly painted, varnished, or lacquered surfaces. Blackout EZ doesn't say how its tape affects paint, so test a small hidden spot first.

**Can I put blackout film on double-pane windows?**

Check the instructions for the specific film. Gila's dark privacy film, for example, is meant to go on the outside surface of sealed dual-pane windows, which is often not practical in a rental.

**Do blackout shades help you sleep better?**

They can cut down the light coming into the room. We can't say they'll change how well you sleep. If trouble sleeping continues, talk to a doctor.
