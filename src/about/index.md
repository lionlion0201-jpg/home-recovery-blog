# About

This site publishes practical, research-based buying guides for home recovery and sleep wellness products — red light therapy, sauna gear, sleep tools, and related categories.

We don't chase every new trend. We focus on categories with steady, long-term demand, and try to write guides that are still useful a year from now, not just this week.
