# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 3, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how QuietRecover ("this site," "we," "us") handles information when you visit quietrecover's website. By using this site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

## Information we collect

**Analytics.** This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, general geographic location (country/city level), device and browser type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Server logs.** Like most websites, standard web server logs may capture IP addresses, browser type, and access times for security and troubleshooting purposes.

**Cookies.** This site itself does not set first-party cookies for tracking purposes beyond what Google Analytics requires. Affiliate links (for example, Amazon Associates links) may set cookies on your device once you click through to the destination retailer's site — those cookies are controlled by the destination site, not by us. See our [Affiliate Disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | url }}) for more on how affiliate links work.

**Information you provide directly.** If you contact us by email, we retain that correspondence only as needed to respond to you.

## How we use information

We use the information above to understand which content is useful to readers, improve the site, and measure the effectiveness of the buying guides we publish. We do not sell visitor data to third parties.

## Third-party services

This site links to and earns commissions through affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, the retailer (e.g., Amazon) handles that transaction and any data associated with it under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit from a link on this page.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, use the Google Analytics opt-out tool linked above, or contact us with any questions or requests regarding your data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
